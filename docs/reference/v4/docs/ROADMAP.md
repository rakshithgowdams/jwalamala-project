# ROADMAP — how to ship an enterprise-level news platform without stalling

Building all 26 phases before launch is the most common way news projects fail. Ship in waves.

| Wave | Phases | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Wave 1 — Launch | 0–14 + P0 parts of 15–17 (trending bar, tags/topics, weather chip, Jain daily times) | Fast, ad-supported Kannada news PWA replacing WordPress |
| Wave 2 — Engage | 18–21, 23 | Jain calendar, series, listen, live blog, community sections, follows, polls, newsletter |
| Wave 3 — Newsroom scale | 22, 24 | Editorial workflow, AI assistant, analytics, social publishing |
| Wave 4 — Advanced | 25–26 | Utility data, web stories, memberships, sponsored products, multi-site |

## Recurring cost drivers to approve with the client
Supabase plan, hosting, weather/AQI commercial plan, TTS characters, AI tokens, SMS OTP, email sending, optional paid market-data API. Each has a budget cap and kill switch in admin.

## Differentiators vs big Kannada news sites
Event-date-first archive and search, Jain daily times and parva calendar, basadi directory, community notices, pravachana series with key points and listen mode, Kannada/English typing search.
